What is the AAISM certification?

The Advanced in AI Security Management (AAISM) is ISACA's first AI-focused security management certification. It is aimed at experienced security professionals — you need a CISM or CISSP to be eligible — and validates your ability to identify, assess, monitor, and mitigate risks specific to enterprise AI systems, from model risk and data misuse to automated decision-making and responsible AI governance.

What does the AAISM exam cover?

The AAISM exam spans three domains: AI Governance and Program Management, AI Risk Management, and AI Technologies and Controls. Together these cover setting policy and oversight for enterprise AI, assessing and treating AI-specific risk against your risk appetite, and applying the technical controls that protect models and data. The free study guides on this page work through each domain, the underlying tasks, common attack types, and the exam-day reasoning you'll need.

How to prepare with this free guide

Start with the comprehensive written study guide to build your foundation across all three domains. Use the exam playbook to understand the blueprint and answering strategy. Watch the video walkthrough to reinforce the key concepts, then test yourself with the 40-question practice quiz — every question includes an explanation. Everything here is free and designed to sit alongside ISACA's official syllabus, not replace it.
