About the exam

What is the AAISM certification?

The Advanced in AI Security Management (AAISM) is ISACA's AI-focused security management certification, aimed at experienced professionals who already hold a CISM or CISSP. It validates your ability to identify, assess, and mitigate risks specific to enterprise AI systems.

What does the AAISM exam cover?

The exam spans three domains: AI governance and program management, AI risk management, and AI technologies and controls. This guide works through each domain with the reasoning you'll need on exam day, alongside a video walkthrough and a 40-question practice quiz with explanations.

How to use this free guide

Start with the written guide to build your foundation, watch the video walkthrough to reinforce the key concepts, then test yourself with the practice quiz. Everything here is free and designed to sit alongside ISACA's official syllabus, not replace it.
